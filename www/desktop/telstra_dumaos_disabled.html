Telstra Internet Optimiser

Internet Optimiser is an extra feature available to eligible broadband plans allowing you to prioritise what's most important to you, whether that be work, play or both using your home internet. Once activated it runs on your Telstra Smart Modem and will put your priority usage first when your home internet is busy.

If you have an eligible service, you can activate Internet Optimiser on this modem via MyTelstra.
